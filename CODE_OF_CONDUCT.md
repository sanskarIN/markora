# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Markora is an open-source project that welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help keep project spaces respectful, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports the project include:

- being respectful when disagreeing about product or engineering decisions;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback rather than attacking people;
- accepting corrections and updating work when evidence changes;
- protecting other people's privacy and removing sensitive information from reports;
- helping newcomers understand project conventions without ridicule;
- staying on topic in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions;
- crediting contributors and sources appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in Markora project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior;
- publishing private information without permission;
- intentionally posting credentials, private documents, malware, or exploit payloads that put others at risk;
- spam, deceptive promotion, or repeated disruption;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

Technical security reports may necessarily discuss vulnerabilities, but they must use the private process in `SECURITY.md` until coordinated disclosure is appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-managed community spaces, and other situations where someone is representing Markora or its maintainers.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently block participation depending on severity and repetition.

When possible, enforcement should be proportional, documented privately, and focused on protecting the community rather than escalating conflict.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For conduct concerns, contact:

- `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`

Do not use a public issue when the report would expose private information. Include enough context to identify the project interaction, but do not forward unrelated personal data.

Security vulnerabilities must instead follow `SECURITY.md`.
